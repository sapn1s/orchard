# FEAT-181 — Auto-escalate tickets that absorb too many verify rounds

## Plan (FILED — build after review)

Wire scripts/round-ceiling.mjs (FEAT-148, already built and actionable via npm run arch:rounds) into an automatic surface so rounds-2+ escalate without being remembered. Source: docs/research/ship-before-verify-2026-10-13.md sec 5(B) and sec 7.

### Why this is FILED as a plan, not built on faith
Cross-provider refutation (OpenAI, run 01a13647-a591-7932-8da6-1de03743628a) rated a round ceiling a notification policy masquerading as a latency intervention UNLESS the escalation has a named owner, a response commitment, and an actionable decision. Named failure modes to design against:
- moving a difficult ticket from an active loop into a slower human queue;
- stopping just before convergence;
- combining several attempts into one round to dodge the ceiling;
- weakening verification to avoid escalation.
So the deliverable is not the ceiling firing; it is the ceiling firing INTO an actionable decision (rescope / redesign / stop), owned by the orchestrator.

### Design sketch
- The reader already exists and refuses to re-derive (reads declared round= only). Options for the surface: (a) board:status prints an over-ceiling section; (b) a dispatch preflight (like the codex-sandbox preflight) warns when dispatching round at or above N on a ticket; (c) both.
- Do NOT auto-file or auto-block (matches arch-watch contract): it RAISES, a human/orchestrator routes.

### Proof bar
- A synthetic cost-ledger fixture at round at or above ceiling raises; below it does not (must-FAIL before).
- The raised item names the declared round count and an actionable choice, verified against a realistic multi-round ledger, not just the minimal case.

### Related
- FEAT-148 (round-ceiling.mjs), FEAT-100 (dispatch declarations), FEAT-086 (cost model), ARCH-017 (11-round exemplar).
